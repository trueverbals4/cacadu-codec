Motivation

The LoRa Bandwidth Problem

  • LoRaWAN transmits kilometres on milliwatts using Chirp Spread Spectrum — but at ~250 bytes per packet
  • A single JPEG frame is 20–100 KB. Direct transmission is impossible.
  • Existing solutions: numeric telemetry only. No images. No CV.
  • Our question: Can a trained ML model compress real camera images into LoRaWAN's budget without destroying downstream CV value?

The answer required solving a compression problem, a security problem, a reliability problem — and discovering an encoder flaw that could silently corrupt ML training data.

Key Discovery

Light Intoxication

⚠ Named Encoder Failure Mode

TruncatedSVD passed the PSNR threshold (35.6 dB > 30 dB broadcast standard) — but its MSE of 17.9 is 4.97× higher than IncrementalPCA's 3.6. The PSNR metric completely missed the failure.

  • Root cause: TruncatedSVD skips mean-centering → brightness variance from auto-exposed highlights dominates leading singular vectors → pixel spatial detail is lost
  • Why it's dangerous: A CNN adapts to the distortion and reports acceptable accuracy — the flaw propagates silently through ML training data
  • Fix: IncrementalPCA mean-centres first → same 12 FPS → 4.97× better MSE

Rate-Distortion Analysis

The 100-Byte Optimum

Fig 2 — MSE minimum at 100B across all encoder types.
  • Below 100B: information irreversibly destroyed
  • At 100B: MSE minimum · PSNR peak · ~99.4% explained variance
  • Above 100B: encoding noise increases faster than signal
  • Key insight: 100B is a property of the data distribution — confirmed independently across ALL 5 encoder architectures. LoRaWAN constraint and data characteristics align at the same optimum.
Production Decision

RGB over Greyscale

Fig 3 — RGB vs greyscale rate-distortion.
  • Greyscale: MSE 17.9, PSNR 35.6 dB — lower raw MSE
  • RGB: MSE 19.1, PSNR 36.3 dB — slightly higher MSE
  • RGB selected because: third spatial channel is essential for downstream real-time image augmentation (Dimension 3: CNN CUDA decoder)
  • Greyscale would require hallucinating colour at the decoder — unquantifiable hallucination error
Production spec: RGB · 100B · PSNR 36.3 dB · MSE 19.1 · CR 122.9:1 · 12 FPS · AES-256-GCM
